Maintainer checklist for preparing version 0.1.1 for public distribution.

- Portable and Codex compatibility manifests, version 0.1.1.
- Category: Education & Research.

## Publisher information still needed

- Intended verified publisher identity and software distribution license.
- Public website, support, privacy-policy, and terms-of-service HTTPS URLs.
- A listing icon and composer icon.
- Intended supported countries and whether any payment or purchase is involved.

Add the confirmed publisher information and assets to the manifests, then rebuild
and validate the archive. Check the submission portal's requirements before
submitting the release. Dark-mode icons and brand colors are optional.
